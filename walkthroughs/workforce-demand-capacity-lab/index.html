Read the narration
  1. Can the hiring plan fit?
    Workforce Demand and Capacity Lab is my portfolio model for testing a hiring plan against recruiting capacity. All scenarios use synthetic data.
  2. Set the operating assumptions.
    Choose a preset, then adjust demand, recruiter availability, agency support, and risk tolerance. Weighted units model recruiting work, not requisition counts.
  3. Find the pressure points.
    The monthly view exposes the pressure points. December is at risk; January and February exceed the base plan’s selected capacity guardrails.
  4. Test added capacity.
    Preview a contract recruiter for January and February. The modeled gap falls from sixteen units to six, but February still exceeds the guardrail.
  5. Compare phasing demand.
    Compare phasing non-critical roles. The preview reduces demand in specified months while preserving the original scenario and modeled priority-zero commitments.
  6. Frame the decision.
    The executive memo summarizes assumptions, remaining gaps, and the decision required. Overall status follows the worst month, so averages cannot hide bottlenecks.
  7. Inspect. Discuss. Decide.
    Review the formulas and limitations, then discuss the tradeoffs with leadership. Explore the Workforce Lab on my portfolio.
