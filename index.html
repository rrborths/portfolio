Recruiting Operating System Assessment

Find what is breaking—and decide what to fix first.

Starting at $7,500 · 3–4 weeks

Hiring demand rarely breaks in one place. The visible symptom may be time to fill, recruiter workload, manager frustration, poor reporting, or an expensive tool that is not changing the work.

The underlying issue is usually that priorities, capacity, process, decisions, data, and systems no longer move together. The assessment gives leadership one evidence base, the material risks, and a sequenced 90-day plan.

Best fit: growing B2B SaaS/technology, HR/workforce technology, and multi-location services companies navigating growth, acquisition, reorganization, a hiring reset, or a systems change.

Start a fit conversation →

01 Diagnose

Executive decision questions, stakeholder interviews, and a current-state review across the operating system behind hiring.

02 Test the evidence

Review workforce priorities, recruiter capacity, process, manager decisions, data, technology, and ownership.

03 Prioritize

Translate findings into a material-risk map and an eight-dimension maturity assessment leadership can act on.

04 Leave with a roadmap

A prioritized 30/60/90 plan with owners, measures, dependencies, and the decisions leadership needs to make next.
