Read the narration
  1. From evidence to decisions.
    Recruiting Control Room is my portfolio prototype for turning recruiting evidence into clear decisions. This walkthrough uses synthetic data.
  2. Define success first.
    Start with structured intake: the business outcome, success profile, required capabilities, interview plan, and capacity constraints.
  3. Make expectations explicit.
    Calibration separates must-have from learnable capabilities, then assigns each interview a decision purpose and accountable owner.
  4. Keep approval visible.
    A named approval gate checks the calibrated definition, owners, dates, and commitments before enabling local kickoff-pack generation.
  5. Find the binding constraint.
    Funnel Health highlights manager-stage friction and connects the bottleneck to evidence, a recommended action, an owner, and a due date.
  6. Run a decision-led review.
    Weekly Review brings changes, decisions, risks, and commitments together, with traceable synthetic sources and approval before local export.
  7. Carry the decision forward.
    Create a browser-local action draft for review. Nothing is sent automatically. Explore Recruiting Control Room on my portfolio.
